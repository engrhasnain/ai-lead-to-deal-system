  WHAT IS THIS PROJECT?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The AI Lead-to-Deal System is an intelligent sales pipeline platform that helps
B2B sales teams turn leads into closed deals — faster and with less manual work.

Think of it as giving every sales rep their own AI research assistant, copywriter,
and sales coach — all running in the background, ready at the click of a button.

The system takes a lead (a potential client) and helps the sales team:
  - Instantly understand who they are and whether they are worth pursuing
  - Know exactly what score (priority level) to give them
  - Know exactly what to do next
  - Send them the right email without writing it from scratch
  - Track the deal through every stage from first contact to closed won
  - Forecast how much revenue to expect and when

  WHAT PROBLEM DOES IT SOLVE?
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

B2B sales is one of the most manual, repetitive, and time-consuming jobs in
any company. Here is the reality for most sales reps today:

  - They spend 2–3 hours researching a new lead before even reaching out
  - They copy-paste a generic email template with minimal personalisation
  - They forget to follow up 3 days later and the lead goes cold
  - They treat a $5,000 deal the same as a $50,000 deal — no prioritisation
  - They have no reliable way to predict when or whether a deal will close
  - Their manager asks "how is the pipeline?" and the honest answer is "I'm not sure"

Studies show B2B sales reps spend only 34% of their time actually selling.
The rest is admin, research, and writing emails.

This system fixes that. It automates the research, scoring, writing, and
forecasting — so reps spend more time talking to people and closing deals.

  WHAT DOES IT ACTUALLY DO? (In Plain English)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. YOU ADD A LEAD
   The sales rep enters basic details: the person's name, email, company,
   job title, industry, and where the lead came from (LinkedIn, referral, etc.).

2. THE AI ENRICHES THE LEAD IN SECONDS
   The AI reads the lead's details and instantly produces:
   → A 2–3 sentence intelligence summary about the company and contact
   → An ICP fit rating: High / Medium / Low
     (ICP = Ideal Customer Profile — how closely they match your ideal buyer)

   Example AI summary: "TechFlow Solutions is a fast-growing SaaS platform
   serving mid-market companies. Sarah Chen is the VP of Sales with direct
   budget authority. Strong ICP match — priority outreach recommended."

3. THE AI SCORES THE LEAD (0–100)
   The AI gives every lead a score based on:
   → How senior their job title is (a CEO scores higher than a manager)
   → How well their industry fits (SaaS/FinTech = high; Manufacturing = low)
   → How large the deal is ($50K+ scores higher than $5K)
   → What stage they are at in the pipeline

   A score of 80+ means: "drop everything and focus here."
   A score of 30 means: "put in a nurture sequence and revisit later."

4. THE AI TELLS YOU WHAT TO DO NEXT
   Instead of wondering "what should I do with this lead?", the AI gives
   one clear, specific next action:
   → "Send personalized cold outreach email today"
   → "Follow up on proposal — no response in 48 hours"
   → "Send final pricing with limited-time incentive"

5. THE AI WRITES THE EMAIL FOR YOU
   With one click, the AI generates a complete, personalised sales email:
   → Cold Outreach     — First contact, professional and value-focused
   → Follow-Up 1       — Adds a data point, softer ask, 3 days after cold
   → Follow-Up 2       — Final polite touch, 7 days after cold
   → Proposal Email    — Formal proposal with 30-day plan and expected outcomes

   Every email uses the lead's actual name, company, role, and AI summary —
   so it reads like it was written specifically for them, not copy-pasted.

6. THE PIPELINE TRACKS EVERY STAGE
   Deals move through 6 stages:
   New → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Won → Lost

   The system tracks how many days each lead has been at each stage.
   If a deal has not moved in 14 days, it gets flagged as "stuck"
   so the sales manager can step in before the deal dies.

7. THE SYSTEM FORECASTS REVENUE
   The AI applies probability weights to every deal:
   → Leads in "New" stage: 5% chance of closing
   → Leads in "Proposal" stage: 40% chance of closing
   → Leads in "Negotiation" stage: 70% chance of closing

   This gives a realistic weighted pipeline forecast and an expected
   quarterly close value — based on data, not gut feel.

8. THE AI WRITES A FULL PIPELINE REPORT
   On demand, the AI reads all the live data and writes a professional
   pipeline intelligence report covering:
   → Executive summary of pipeline health
   → Stage distribution analysis
   → Top opportunities to focus on
   → Specific recommended actions for today, this week, this month
