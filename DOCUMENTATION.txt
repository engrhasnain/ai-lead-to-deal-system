  1. THE PROBLEM
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Every B2B sales team faces the same core problem: too many leads, not enough
intelligence about them, and too much manual work to manage the pipeline effectively.

Here is what a typical sales rep's day looks like without this system:

  - Spends 2–3 hours researching a new lead on LinkedIn, their website, news articles
  - Copy-pastes a generic cold email template and slightly personalises it
  - Adds the lead to a spreadsheet or CRM with no scoring or prioritisation
  - Forgets to follow up 3 days later because there is no prompt
  - Has no idea which leads in the pipeline are most likely to close
  - Reports to the sales manager with a gut-feel pipeline forecast — not data

The result?

  → High-value leads get treated the same as low-value ones — time is wasted
  → Outreach emails feel generic and get ignored
  → Deals stall in the pipeline because no one knows what to do next
  → Pipeline forecasts are guesses, not data-backed projections
  → Win rates are lower than they should be because follow-up timing is wrong
  → Sales managers have no reliable visibility into pipeline health
  → Revenue targets are missed because the funnel leaks at every stage

This is not a small problem. Studies show B2B sales reps spend only 34% of their
time actually selling — the rest is admin, research, and manual CRM work.

  2. WHAT THIS SYSTEM SOLVES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

The AI Lead-to-Deal System is an end-to-end intelligent sales pipeline platform
that replaces manual research, generic outreach, and gut-feel forecasting with
AI-driven intelligence at every stage of the sales process.

Instead of a sales rep guessing what to do, the AI:

  ✦  ENRICHES every lead automatically — generating a company and contact
     summary plus an ICP (Ideal Customer Profile) fit rating in seconds

  ✦  SCORES every lead 0–100 based on title seniority, industry fit, deal size,
     and pipeline stage — so reps know exactly where to focus

  ✦  RECOMMENDS the specific next action for each lead — one precise step,
     not a vague suggestion

  ✦  GENERATES personalized outreach emails — cold emails, follow-ups,
     and formal proposals — tailored to the lead's role, company, and context

  ✦  TRACKS the full activity timeline — every enrichment, scoring event,
     email sent, stage change, and note — in one view

  ✦  FORECASTS pipeline revenue — probability-weighted projections per stage,
     expected quarterly close value, and lost deal reason analysis

  ✦  PRODUCES AI-written pipeline reports — executive summaries, health analysis,
     top opportunities, and prioritised recommended actions

In short: this system gives every sales rep the intelligence of a senior analyst
and the writing ability of a professional copywriter — on demand, for every lead.

  4. KEY FEATURES AT A GLANCE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  LEAD MANAGEMENT
  ├─ Create, update, view, and delete leads
  ├─ Track lead source: LinkedIn, Referral, Website, Conference, Cold Outreach
  ├─ Assign deal value and currency per lead
  ├─ Add free-form notes and lost reasons
  ├─ Auto-detect stuck leads (>14 days in same stage)
  └─ Full activity timeline per lead

  PIPELINE STAGE TRACKING
  ├─ 6 stages: New → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Won → Lost
  ├─ Days-in-stage tracked automatically per stage entry
  ├─ Stage changes logged to activity timeline with timestamps
  └─ Stuck deal alerts when a lead has not progressed in 14+ days

  AI LEAD ENRICHMENT
  ├─ Generates a 2–3 sentence company and contact intelligence summary
  ├─ Assigns ICP fit: High / Medium / Low
  ├─ Considers title seniority, industry, and company context
  └─ Runs in seconds — replaces hours of manual research

  AI LEAD SCORING (0–100)
  ├─ Scores based on: title seniority (40%), industry fit (30%),
  │  deal size (20%), stage progression (10%)
  ├─ Returns a score + 2-sentence written reasoning
  ├─ Recommends the appropriate pipeline stage based on score
  └─ Score thresholds: 75+ = High Priority, 50–74 = Medium, <50 = Low

  AI NEXT-ACTION RECOMMENDATION
  ├─ Reads stage, score, and industry context
  ├─ Returns one specific, actionable next step (max 12 words)
  └─ Examples: "Send final pricing with limited-time incentive" or
               "Schedule 30-min discovery call this week"

  AI EMAIL GENERATION
  ├─ 4 email types:
  │   Cold Outreach    → First contact, value-proposition focused
  │   Follow-Up 1     → 3 days after cold, adds social proof
  │   Follow-Up 2     → 7 days, final polite touch
  │   Proposal Email  → Formal, structured proposal with 30-day plan
  ├─ Every email personalised to lead name, company, title, and AI summary
  ├─ Returns full subject line and formatted email body
  └─ Email open tracking (opened_at timestamp)

  DASHBOARD
  ├─ Total leads in pipeline
  ├─ Pipeline value (all non-won, non-lost deals)
  ├─ Revenue won this month and this quarter
  ├─ AI actions counter (enrichments + scores + emails)
  ├─ Quota progress tracker
  ├─ Deals closing this week (proposal + negotiation leads, sorted by score)
  ├─ Top 5 leads by score
  └─ Recent activity feed (last 8 events across all leads)

  PIPELINE ANALYTICS
  ├─ Lead count and deal value by stage
  ├─ Win rate percentage
  ├─ Average deal size
  ├─ Leads by source (LinkedIn, Referral, etc.)
  ├─ Leads by industry
  ├─ High ICP count and scored leads count
  └─ Visual charts for all metrics

  REVENUE FORECAST
  ├─ Probability-weighted pipeline value per stage:
  │   New=5%, Qualified=20%, Proposal=40%, Negotiation=70%, Won=100%
  ├─ Expected close value this quarter
  ├─ Stage-by-stage weighted forecast breakdown
  └─ Lost deal reason analysis (Price, Competitor, No Budget, etc.)

  AI PIPELINE REPORT
  ├─ AI-written full markdown report on demand
  ├─ Sections: Executive Summary, Pipeline Health, Top Opportunities,
  │  Recommended Actions
  ├─ Uses live pipeline data to generate specific, data-backed insights
  └─ Printable / exportable format

  ACTIVITY TIMELINE (per lead)
  ├─ Event types: created, enriched, scored, email_sent, email_opened,
  │  stage_change, next_action, note_added
  ├─ Full description and timestamp per event
  └─ Sorted newest-first, full history preserved

  5. HOW IT WORKS — IMPLEMENTATION FLOW
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Below is the complete step-by-step flow of how a lead moves through the system
from first entry to closed deal.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STEP 1 — LEAD ENTRY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A new lead is added to the system with the following data:

  Required fields:
    • Full name
    • Email address
    • Company name
    • Job title / role
    • Industry
    • Lead source (LinkedIn / Referral / Website / Conference / Cold Outreach)

  Optional fields:
    • Deal value (e.g., $45,000 USD)
    • Notes

  On creation:
    • Lead enters the pipeline at stage: NEW
    • Stage entry timestamp recorded
    • Activity logged: "Lead created from [source]"

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STEP 2 — AI LEAD ENRICHMENT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The user clicks "Enrich" on any lead. The AI runs instantly and returns:

  • AI Summary: A 2–3 sentence company and contact intelligence brief
    Example: "TechFlow Solutions is a fast-growing SaaS platform serving
    mid-market companies. Sarah Chen is the VP of Sales with direct budget
    authority. Strong ICP match — priority outreach recommended."

  • ICP Fit:
      High   → Senior title + core ICP industry (SaaS, FinTech, Healthcare,
                E-Commerce, Logistics, Technology)
      Medium → Senior title OR core ICP industry, but not both
      Low    → Junior title + non-core industry

  The enrichment timestamp is saved and the activity log is updated.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STEP 3 — AI LEAD SCORING
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The user clicks "Score" on any lead. The AI evaluates the lead and returns:

  Scoring criteria:
    • Title seniority   40% — CEO/Founder/CTO = max; Manager = mid; etc.
    • Industry fit      30% — SaaS/FinTech = max; Manufacturing = low
    • Deal size         20% — $50K+ = max; <$15K = low
    • Stage progression 10% — later stage = higher signal

  Output:
    • Score: 0–100 integer
    • Score Reasoning: 2-sentence plain-English explanation
    • Recommended Stage: where this lead should ideally sit

  Score interpretation:
    75–100 → High priority — senior decision-maker, strong ICP, fast-track
    50–74  → Medium priority — needs qualification before advancing
    0–49   → Low priority — long sales cycle expected, nurture recommended

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STEP 4 — AI NEXT-ACTION RECOMMENDATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The user requests a next-action suggestion for any lead. The AI reads:
  • Current pipeline stage
  • Lead score
  • Industry context

And returns ONE specific action in plain English (max 12 words):

  Stage examples:
    New:         "Send personalized cold outreach email today"
    Qualified:   "Schedule 30-min discovery call this week"
    Proposal:    "Follow up on proposal — no response in 48 hours"
    Negotiation: "Send final pricing with limited-time incentive"
    Won:         "Send onboarding schedule and welcome kit"
    Lost:        "Add to 90-day nurture sequence for re-engagement"

  The recommended action is saved to the lead and shown prominently on the lead card.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STEP 5 — AI EMAIL GENERATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The user selects an email type and clicks "Generate Email." The AI writes a
complete, personalised email using all available lead context.

  Email types and their purpose:
    Cold Outreach   → First contact. References the company's trajectory, shows
                      specific value props (AI enrichment, scoring, outreach),
                      and requests a 20-minute call or Loom video.

    Follow-Up 1     → Sent 3 days after cold email. Adds a data point
                      (e.g., "a client in your industry reduced deal cycles
                      from 52 to 31 days"). More specific ask.

    Follow-Up 2     → Sent 7 days after cold. Final polite touch.
                      States ROI (3.2x average in 6 months).
                      No pressure — leaves the door open.

    Proposal Email  → Formal proposal. Includes a 30-day implementation plan,
                      expected 90-day outcomes (45% less manual research,
                      30% more qualified pipeline, 25% higher close rate),
                      and requests a call to walk through it.

  Each email includes:
    • A personalised subject line
    • A full email body with name, company, and context woven in
    • Saved to the database and linked to the lead
    • Open tracking (opened_at timestamp when marked)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STEP 6 — STAGE PROGRESSION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

As the lead advances through conversations and meetings, the user updates
the pipeline stage. The system:

  • Records the stage change timestamp (stage_entered_at)
  • Logs the transition to the activity timeline:
    "Moved from Qualified → Proposal"
  • Calculates days_in_stage from stage entry to now
  • Flags the lead as STUCK if it has been in the same stage for 14+ days
    without progressing

  Full pipeline progression:
    NEW → QUALIFIED → PROPOSAL → NEGOTIATION → WON
                                              → LOST (with lost_reason)

  Lost reasons tracked: Price, Competitor, No Budget, Ghosted, Timeline, Other

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STEP 7 — PIPELINE ANALYTICS
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The analytics page gives a real-time view of the entire pipeline:

  • Total leads and total deal value across all stages
  • Win rate (won ÷ all closed × 100)
  • Average deal size
  • Lead count and value broken down by every stage
  • Lead count by source (LinkedIn vs Referral vs Website, etc.)
  • Lead count by industry (SaaS vs FinTech vs Healthcare, etc.)
  • Count of High ICP leads
  • Count of scored leads

  All data visualised as bar charts and summary cards on the analytics page.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STEP 8 — REVENUE FORECAST
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The forecast applies probability weights to every deal in the pipeline:

  Stage probabilities:
    New          → 5%  probability of closing
    Qualified    → 20% probability of closing
    Proposal     → 40% probability of closing
    Negotiation  → 70% probability of closing
    Won          → 100% (already closed)
    Lost         → 0%

  Output:
    • Weighted pipeline value (sum of all deals × their stage probability)
    • Expected close value this quarter (weighted × 0.75 buffer)
    • Per-stage breakdown: raw value, weighted value, probability %, deal count
    • Lost deal reason breakdown — shows why deals are lost (Price, Competitor, etc.)

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  STEP 9 — AI PIPELINE REPORT
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

On demand, the AI generates a full written pipeline intelligence report using
live analytics data. Sections include:

  ## Executive Summary
    → Overall pipeline health in plain English, total leads, value, win rate

  ## Pipeline Health
    → Stage distribution analysis, ICP breakdown, qualification efficiency

  ## Top Opportunities
    → Which deals to focus on, which verticals close fastest, proposal strategy

  ## Recommended Actions
    → Today / This Week / This Month / Ongoing — specific, prioritised steps

  The report is rendered in formatted markdown and is printable/exportable.

  9. DATABASE STRUCTURE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  TABLE: leads
  ├─ id, name, email, company, title, industry
  ├─ source  (LinkedIn / Referral / Website / Conference / Cold Outreach / manual)
  ├─ stage   (new / qualified / proposal / negotiation / won / lost)
  ├─ score   (0–100, nullable), score_reasoning (text)
  ├─ deal_value (float), currency (default: USD)
  ├─ next_action (text, nullable)
  ├─ ai_summary  (text, nullable)
  ├─ icp_fit     (high / medium / low, nullable)
  ├─ enriched_at (datetime, nullable)
  ├─ notes       (text, nullable)
  ├─ lost_reason (text, nullable)
  ├─ stage_entered_at (datetime)
  └─ created_at, updated_at

  TABLE: emails
  ├─ id, lead_id (FK → leads)
  ├─ email_type  (cold / follow_up_1 / follow_up_2 / proposal)
  ├─ subject     (string)
  ├─ body        (text)
  ├─ generated_at (datetime)
  └─ opened_at   (datetime, nullable)

  TABLE: lead_activities
  ├─ id, lead_id (FK → leads)
  ├─ event_type  (created / enriched / scored / email_sent / email_opened /
  │               stage_change / next_action / note_added)
  ├─ description (string, max 500 chars)
  └─ created_at  (datetime)

  RELATIONSHIPS:
  Lead → Emails          (one lead has many generated emails)
  Lead → LeadActivities  (one lead has a full activity history)

  11. PIPELINE STAGES EXPLAINED
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  NEW
  → Lead just entered the system. Not yet enriched or scored.
  → AI action: Enrich → Score → Get Next Action → Send Cold Email
  → Close probability: 5%

  QUALIFIED
  → Lead has been researched, scored, and confirmed worth pursuing.
  → Discovery call completed or scheduled.
  → AI action: Schedule call / Send ROI case study
  → Close probability: 20%

  PROPOSAL
  → Formal proposal sent to the lead.
  → Awaiting response and negotiation.
  → AI action: Follow up / Address objections / Walk through proposal
  → Close probability: 40%

  NEGOTIATION
  → Deal is being actively negotiated. Pricing, terms, and timeline being finalised.
  → AI action: Send final pricing / Loop in legal / Confirm go-live timeline
  → Close probability: 70%

  WON
  → Deal is closed. Payment or contract signed.
  → AI action: Send onboarding schedule / Introduce CSM / Schedule kickoff call
  → Close probability: 100%

  LOST
  → Deal did not close. Reason recorded.
  → Lost reasons: Price / Competitor / No Budget / Ghosted / Timeline
  → AI action: Add to 90-day nurture sequence / Request loss reason feedback
  → Close probability: 0%

  STUCK DEAL DETECTION:
  → Any lead in new / qualified / proposal / negotiation for more than 14 days
    without a stage change is automatically flagged as STUCK.
  → These appear highlighted in the pipeline view for immediate attention.

  12. DEMO MODE VS. LIVE MODE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

  DEMO MODE (DEMO_MODE=true, default):
  ─────────────────────────────────────
  → AI enrichment uses rule-based ICP classification (title + industry matching)
  → AI scoring uses a points-based algorithm across title, industry, deal value
  → Next-action uses stage-based pre-written action suggestions
  → Emails use polished pre-written templates personalised with lead name/company
  → Pipeline report uses a structured template with live data inserted
  → No API key required — everything works out of the box
  → Pre-loaded with 12 realistic demo leads across all stages

  LIVE MODE (ANTHROPIC_API_KEY set):
  ────────────────────────────────────
  → All AI tasks call Claude (claude-sonnet-4-6) directly
  → Enrichment summaries are fully contextual and dynamic
  → Scoring reasoning adapts to every unique lead combination
  → Emails are genuinely personalised to each lead's specific context
  → Pipeline reports are written fresh from real-time analytics data

  Environment variables:
    ANTHROPIC_API_KEY  → Claude API access key
    DEMO_MODE          → Set to "false" to enable live AI mode

  ✓ Stuck deals flagged automatically — managers intervene before it's too late
