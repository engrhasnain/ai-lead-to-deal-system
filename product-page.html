The Problem

B2B sales is broken
by manual work.

Your reps are spending most of their day on research, writing, and admin — not on talking to people and closing deals.

  • 🔍

    Hours lost to manual research

    Reps spend 2–3 hours per lead researching LinkedIn, company websites, and news — before even reaching out.

  • 📧

    Generic emails get ignored

    Copy-pasted templates with minimal personalisation. The prospect can tell instantly — and they don't reply.

  • 📊

    No lead prioritisation

    A $5,000 lead gets the same attention as a $50,000 one. There is no scoring, no ranking, just a list of names.

  • 😶

    Deals go cold silently

    Reps forget to follow up. Leads go cold. Nobody notices until the quarter is over and targets are missed.

  • 📉

    Pipeline forecasts are guesses

    "How confident are you?" — the honest answer is "not very." Forecasting from a spreadsheet is not forecasting.

How It Works

From first lead to closed deal —
8 intelligent steps.

AI assists at every step of the journey — so reps spend their time on conversations, not admin.

1

Add the Lead

Enter the lead's name, company, title, industry, and source. That's it — the system handles the rest.

2

AI Enriches the Lead in Seconds

The AI generates a 2–3 sentence intelligence brief and assigns an ICP fit rating — High, Medium, or Low. Replaces 2–3 hours of manual research.

AI EnrichmentICP: High / Medium / Low
3

AI Scores the Lead (0–100)

Score is calculated from title seniority (40%), industry fit (30%), deal value (20%), and stage (10%). Returns a score plus a 2-sentence written explanation.

AI Scoring75+ = High Priority50–74 = Medium
4

AI Recommends the Next Action

One specific, actionable step based on stage, score, and industry. Never wonder "what should I do with this lead?" again.

AI Next-ActionMax 12 words, instantly actionable
5

AI Writes the Email

Select an email type and click Generate. The AI writes a complete, personalised email using the lead's name, company, role, and AI summary.

Pipeline Stage Progression

New → Qualified → Proposal → Negotiation → Won
